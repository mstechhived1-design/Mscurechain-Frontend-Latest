Privacy & Security

Global Data Protection Protocols v3.2

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account on the MS CureChain Patient App, we collect personal details including your Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and encrypted Password. We also maintain profile information such as your Date of Birth, Gender, and Blood Group to provide tailored healthcare services.

2. Prominent Disclosure: App Permissions

To provide essential digital healthcare features, our mobile application requires specific permissions. We value your privacy and only request access when necessary for the core functionality of the app.

Camera Permission (Required for Booking)

This app requires access to your camera specifically to scan QR codes for quick doctor discovery and appointment booking. It is also used to capture photos of medical documents for your personal health records. No data is stored or transmitted unless you choose to upload a document.

Location Permission

Used to help you find nearby hospitals and clinics based on your current geographical area to facilitate your healthcare journey.

Storage Permission

Used to allow you to securely download and save medical reports, prescriptions, and lab results directly to your device.

Notification Permission

Used to send critical health alerts, appointment reminders, and status updates regarding your medical records.

3. Data Sovereignty & Security

All patient and hospital data entered into MS CureChain belongs exclusively to the patient and the respective hospital. We implement AES-256 bit encryption for all patient-identifiable data at rest and in transit.

4. Data Usage & Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal health data. Data is shared only with healthcare providers you book appointments with or when required by government regulatory bodies (like ABDM).

5. Contact & Legal

If you have any questions regarding our data practices, please contact privacy@mscurechain.com.
